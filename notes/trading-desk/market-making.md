# Market making

A market maker continuously quotes a bid and an offer, earning the [[Bid-ask spread]] in exchange for taking on [[Inventory risk]].

Two enemies:
- **Inventory** — getting stuck long into a falling market.
- **[[Adverse selection]]** — trading with someone who knows more than you.

Good quoting *skews* prices: when long, lower both bid and ask to attract buyers and shed inventory.
