# Inventory risk

The risk a dealer carries between buying from one client and selling to another. Measured in the book's [[The Greeks|Greeks]] for options, or in DV01 / beta-adjusted notional for linear products.

Managed by skewing quotes ([[Market making]]), hedging in the street, or internalising offsetting flow.
