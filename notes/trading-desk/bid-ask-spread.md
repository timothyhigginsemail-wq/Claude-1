# Bid-ask spread

The difference between the best offer and the best bid. For a taker it's a cost; for a [[Market making|market maker]] it's revenue.

Spread compensates for order-processing cost, [[Inventory risk]], and [[Adverse selection]]. Wider in volatile and illiquid names.
