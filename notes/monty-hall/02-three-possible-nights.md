# The three possible nights

(you always pick door 1)

* Car behind door 1 (yours): the host opens door 2 or 3, whichever he likes. The exposed door stays shut by chance, so it's a decoy.
* Car behind door 2: the host has to open door 3. The exposed door stays shut because the car is there.
* Car behind door 3: the host has to open door 2. The exposed door stays shut because the car is there.
