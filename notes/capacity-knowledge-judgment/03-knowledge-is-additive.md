# Knowledge is additive and effectively unbounded.

Every correct understanding adds to what we know. Some knowledge is more valuable than others, but there is no fixed ceiling on how much understanding we can accumulate. Ignorance is the baseline: knowing something earns positive credit, while not knowing it earns zero, not a deduction.
