# Capacity, knowledge, and judgment follow different scoring principles.

Capacity is constrained by biology. Knowledge is an accumulating stock of correct understanding, with no fixed scoring ceiling. Judgment is refined toward an ideal of sound reasoning and accurate calibration, where weaknesses can count against someone. The goal is neither certainty nor skepticism, but believing what the evidence supports with the confidence it warrants.
