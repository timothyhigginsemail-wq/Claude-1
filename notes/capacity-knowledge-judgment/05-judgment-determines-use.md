# Judgment determines how effectively we use our capacity and knowledge.

It involves identifying relevant evidence, reasoning logically, assigning appropriate confidence, and updating beliefs when new information emerges. High intelligence and extensive knowledge do not guarantee good judgment; both can be used to rationalize existing biases. Conversely, sound judgment requires enough knowledge to recognize what constitutes meaningful evidence.
