# Poor judgment is subtractive in a way that ignorance is not.

Not knowing something is neutral, but unjustified certainty, flawed reasoning, and resistance to contradictory evidence warrant deductions. Someone can reach the right conclusion through poor reasoning or the wrong conclusion through sound reasoning. Judgment should therefore be evaluated by the quality of the thinking, not simply the correctness of the outcome.
