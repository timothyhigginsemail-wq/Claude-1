# Intellectual ability has three distinct components: capacity, knowledge, and judgment.

Capacity is our biological ability to process information, recognize patterns, and reason. Knowledge is our accumulated understanding of reality. Judgment is how effectively we evaluate evidence, assess uncertainty, form conclusions, and revise beliefs.
