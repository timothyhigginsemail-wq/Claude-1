# Being wrong is a natural part of learning.

We necessarily rely on incomplete information and simplified models to understand the world, making incorrect beliefs unavoidable. A mistake often reflects knowledge not yet acquired rather than limited capacity or poor judgment. What matters is not simply whether a belief is correct, but how reasonably it was formed, held, and revised.
