# Capacity is biologically constrained; knowledge and judgment are developed.

People are born with different cognitive abilities and natural tendencies in how they think. Yet everyone begins knowing almost nothing about an extraordinarily complex world, with judgment initially driven largely by intuition. Knowledge must be acquired, and judgment must be refined through learning and experience. Neither ignorance nor limited natural ability is an intellectual failure.
