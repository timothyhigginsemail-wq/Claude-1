# The Greeks

| Greek | Sensitivity to | Intuition |
|---|---|---|
| Delta Δ | spot | hedge ratio |
| Gamma Γ | spot (2nd order) | how fast delta changes |
| Vega ν | [[Implied volatility]] | vol exposure |
| Theta Θ | time | the rent you pay for gamma |

Long gamma ⇔ short theta. This trade-off is the whole of [[Gamma scalping]].

On the desk, Greeks aggregate into the book's [[Inventory risk]].
