# Volatility smile

[[Implied volatility]] is not flat across strikes. Equity indices show a *skew* (OTM puts expensive); FX often a symmetric smile.

Ideas to develop: crash-o-phobia since 1987, sticky-strike vs sticky-delta, [[Heston model]], [[SABR model]].
