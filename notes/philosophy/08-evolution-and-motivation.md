# Evolution and motivation

**Step 8 of 9 · Understanding ourselves**

> Evolution shaped our drives toward survival, reproduction, pleasure, attachment, belonging, status, autonomy, and exploration, but their evolutionary origins do not establish which goals we should pursue.

Explicitly respects the [[Is-ought gap]]: explaining where a desire comes from doesn't justify it.
