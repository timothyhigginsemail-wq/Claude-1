# Prediction and reliability

**Step 5 of 9 · Establishing understanding**

> Observed regularities allow us to construct models whose reliability can be evaluated through their predictive success, although past success cannot guarantee future results.

Open question: judging reliability by past success is itself induction — the [[Problem of induction]]. A possible answer is the [[Pragmatic vindication of induction]]: if any method works, this one will.
