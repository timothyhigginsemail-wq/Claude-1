# Existence and uncertainty

**Step 1 of 9 · Establishing understanding**

> Something exists—at minimum, experience is occurring—but we cannot conclusively establish its ultimate nature.

The minimal starting point. Note that this establishes *experience*, not yet a "we": the existence of a subject and of [[Other minds]] has to be earned later.
