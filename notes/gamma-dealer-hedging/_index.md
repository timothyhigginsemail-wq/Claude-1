# The Core Intuition of Gamma & Dealer Hedging

* Delta = Speed (Rate of Gain/Loss): How many dollars your option makes or loses for a 1-point move in the underlying index.
* Gamma = Acceleration (Change in Delta): How fast your Delta shifts as the index moves. Gamma peaks right at the strike price and explodes as time to expiration approaches zero (0DTE).
