# Why Selling ANY Option = Short Gamma (-Gamma)

When you sell an option (Call or Put), you collect upfront cash to act as the insurance company: your profit caps out when you are right, and your liability snowballs when you are wrong.

* On a Rally (Price Goes UP):
   * Short Call: You lose increasingly more money per point as the market rises (Delta drops: -20 → -50 → -100).
   * Short Put: You make increasingly less money per point as the Put expires worthless and your upside caps out (Delta drops: +100 → +50 → 0).
* The Number-Line Rule: Whether you sold a Call or a Put, when the market goes up, your Delta goes down; when the market goes down, your Delta goes up.
