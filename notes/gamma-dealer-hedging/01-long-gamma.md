# Why Owning ANY Option = Long Gamma (+Gamma)

When you buy an option (Call or Put), you pay an upfront premium so that your bet automatically gets bigger when you are right and shrinks toward zero when you are wrong.

* On a Rally (Price Goes UP):
   * Long Call: You make increasingly more money per point as the market rises (Delta climbs: +20 → +50 → +100).
   * Long Put: You lose increasingly less money per point as the market rises and the Put fades toward zero (Delta climbs up the number line: -100 → -50 → 0).
* The Number-Line Rule: Moving from -50 to -20 is an increase of +30. Whether you own a Call or a Put, when the market goes up, your Delta goes up; when the market goes down, your Delta goes down.
