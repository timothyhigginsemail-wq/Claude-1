Inner London is safe to walk around by big-city standards. The most common risk is phone snatching, often from a bike. Red flags mark a named local problem, not an unsafe area. Tap any flag for the detail and its source.

Zones are indicative: London neighbourhoods have no official boundaries. Kinds are our read of researched notes on each area. Every flag links to its source; match-day flags are general knowledge.
